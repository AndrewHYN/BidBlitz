# Buyer Protection and Delivery

**Audience:** buyers and sellers on BidBlitz, and the operators who apply it.

This describes what BidBlitz actually does today. It is not legal advice and it
does not invent deadlines, guarantees or rights that have not been confirmed
with a lawyer and with Paynow. Where a rule belongs to Paynow rather than to
BidBlitz, it says so.

---

## 1. Your bid is a commitment

A bid on BidBlitz is a firm commitment to buy that item at that amount. Bids
cannot be withdrawn, including by accident and including by bidding on the
wrong auction.

- Every bid must be at least the current bid plus the auction's bid increment.
- If you win, you are expected to pay. A win you do not want is still a win.
- If someone bids in the final protected window, the auction is extended
  automatically to give other bidders a fair chance. That happens on BidBlitz's
  servers, not in your browser.
- The current price, the winner and the closing time all come from BidBlitz's
  servers. The countdown on your screen is a convenience, not the authority.

If you cannot pay, contact us as soon as you know. That is always better than
going quiet, and it is treated as a normal situation rather than a dispute
against you.

---

## 2. What you pay

If you win an auction at **$25.00**:

```
Winning bid                                    $25.00
+ Paynow payment charge    (shown by Paynow)   $  —    ← Paynow's own charge
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
= Total you authorise with Paynow               $  —
```

The Paynow payment charge is what Paynow charges for the payment method. It is
calculated and displayed by Paynow on its own payment page before you confirm
anything. **BidBlitz does not set it, does not keep it, and does not receive
it.** It is not a BidBlitz fee.

The 5% platform fee is **not** added to your bill. It is taken out of the
seller's side of the sale.

You will see the winning price, the platform fee and the seller's proceeds on
the transaction record, so both sides always see the same numbers.

---

## 3. Paying

- A **Pay now** button appears on a won sale only while a payment provider is
  connected. If you do not see one, you are not being charged.
- You are taken to Paynow's page. Enter your details there, not on BidBlitz.
- The sale is recorded as **Paid** only when Paynow's own signed confirmation
  reaches BidBlitz and passes signature, amount, currency and reference checks.
  Being redirected back to BidBlitz does **not** mark a sale paid, and nothing
  you type into a BidBlitz form can mark a sale paid.
- If a payment is cancelled or fails, the sale is recorded as **Failed** and you
  are not charged.

If you have paid and the sale still says *Awaiting payment*, use **Check
status** on the transaction. That asks Paynow directly and tells you the real
position. It is safe to press more than once.

---

## 4. "Paid" is not "the seller has been paid"

These are two different records on purpose, and it is worth being precise
about them:

| What you see | What it actually means |
| --- | --- |
| **Payment: Paid** | Paynow has confirmed *your* payment |
| **Payout: Waiting for fulfilment** | The seller has not been paid yet |

Sellers are paid after they have delivered the item and after the buyer's
opportunity to raise a problem has passed. BidBlitz records the seller's payout
as a separate step and shows it on the seller's dashboard. It is not
transferred to the seller at the moment you pay.

---

## 5. What sellers must do

If you win an auction, you must deliver or hand over the item that was listed.

Sellers are responsible for:

- **Accurate descriptions.** The title, description and photos are the promise.
  A materially wrong description is a refundable event.
- **Accurate photos.** Of the actual item.
- **The actual item.** No substitutions without the buyer's agreement.
- **Communication.** Responding to the buyer's messages, and telling them
  promptly if something goes wrong.

Sellers who win a sale but cannot or will not fulfil it can have their payout
held, can have the buyer refunded, and can have their account suspended.

---

## 6. Delivery, pickup and acceptable evidence

BidBlitz does not ship anything and does not courier anything. Fulfilment is
between the buyer and the seller, and the operator records what happened.

**If the listing is for pickup:** the handover in person is the fulfilment
event.

**If the listing is for delivery:** agree the delivery address with the buyer
*before* anything is dispatched.

Evidence that will be accepted:

- **In-person handover** — confirmed by an operator or a named witness, with a
  date and time.
- **Courier or tracked delivery** — the courier's tracking reference plus proof
  of delivery.
- **Written confirmation from both parties** — email, WhatsApp or SMS, kept
  with the sale.

Evidence that will **not** be accepted on its own:

- "The seller said so."
- An unprovenanced screenshot.
- A delivery notification sent to an address the buyer never confirmed.

If you are a buyer and you received the wrong or damaged item, **photograph it
before you do anything else with it**, keep the packaging, and tell us straight
away. Evidence that is captured the same day is worth a great deal; evidence
reconstructed a week later is worth very little.

---

## 7. Raising a problem

If something is wrong, tell us. You do not have to resolve it with the seller
first.

Include, if you can:

- the transaction, or the auction URL
- what you expected and what you actually got
- photographs
- your Paynow reference and a screenshot of your payment
- anything the seller has told you, in writing if you have it

**Raising a problem blocks the seller's payout.** That is deliberate: it is
there so a seller cannot be paid while a buyer is still waiting.

If a problem is resolved in the seller's favour, the payout resumes. If it is
resolved in yours, the payout stays held and the money is returned to you
through a manual refund. `docs/MARKETPLACE_OPERATIONS.md` describes exactly how
that is done and what is recorded.

We do not quote you a legal deadline for raising a problem, because none has
been confirmed for this platform. What we do say is that the sooner you tell us,
the better your position and the seller's. A problem raised in days is easy to
put right. One raised in months is not.

---

## 8. What happens to a disputed sale

1. The payout is held immediately. The seller is not paid.
2. Both sides are asked for their account of what happened.
3. Delivery evidence and the listing as published are checked.
4. The usual outcomes are a refund to you, a partial refund, or the payout
   resuming to the seller if the claim does not stand.
5. Whichever way it goes, the decision and its reason are recorded on the sale.

A dispute is not a way to keep an item and get your money back. If a claim does
not stand, the payout proceeds.

---

## 9. Non-delivery, misrepresentation, damage

| What happened | What BidBlitz does |
| --- | --- |
| Item never arrived, and the seller stops responding | Refund the buyer; payout held; seller loses the sale |
| Item is materially not as described | Refund the buyer; payout held; listing reviewed |
| Item arrived damaged | Partial or full refund, or the parties agree a repair or return |
| Wrong item sent | Treated as not as described |
| Buyer will not collect a pickup item | Held, then escalated to a human; never auto-released |
| Seller is unresponsive generally | Payout held; account reviewed |

In every case the money BidBlitz holds is what came from the buyer through
Paynow, less Paynow's own charge. A refund returns what was collected.

---

## 10. Escalation

Escalate to a human — do not keep going round in messages — when:

- The seller is not responding.
- The dispute is about money already moved.
- Anyone asks you to pay outside BidBlitz, or to change a payment or payout
  record.
- You believe an account is impersonating another seller, or has copied a
  listing including the photos.
- You suspect a payment you did not authorise.

**Never** send money to "release" an item, a payout or a refund. No legitimate
process requires it, and it is the most common scam against auction buyers.

## 11. How this interacts with Paynow BuySafe

*This section describes Paynow's own process, which BidBlitz does not control
and has not been able to observe end to end. It is included so the relationship
between a Paynow payment and a BidBlitz sale is not a surprise.*

- Paynow offers a buyer-protection product marketed as BuySafe on its payment
  pages. Whether and how it appears is decided by **Paynow**, on **Paynow's**
  payment page, for **Paynow's** transaction.
- BuySafe is a relationship between the **buyer and Paynow**. It is not a
  BidBlitz guarantee, it does not extend BidBlitz's own dispute handling, and
  it does not change what BidBlitz records on a sale.
- If you use it, follow Paynow's own process and its own timelines for it. We
  cannot give you a BuySafe deadline, because it is not ours to give.
- If a Paynow payment shows a status BidBlitz has not yet received — a
  transaction Paynow calls `Disputed` or `Refunded` — BidBlitz records that in
  the payment audit log and does not guess what it means. Ask us, and we will
  check Paynow's signed status directly.

A BuySafe claim does not pause BidBlitz's fulfilment clock automatically. If a
payment is genuinely in dispute, tell us and the seller payout is held.

---

## 12. Payout holds: what they mean for you as a buyer

You will sometimes see that a sale is complete for you but that the seller's
payout is on hold. That is normal and it is not a sign of anything wrong with
your payment.

It means a hold or a dispute has been recorded against the seller's payout
while it is looked into. Your payment is unaffected by it, and the money you
paid is what BidBlitz is holding.

---

## 13. If you are a seller

Your proceeds are the winning price minus the 5% BidBlitz platform fee. Paynow's
payment charge is not yours and is not deducted twice.

Your proceeds are paid to you **after**:

1. the buyer has paid,
2. you have delivered or handed over the item,
3. the buyer's opportunity to raise a problem has passed, and
4. no dispute is open.

You can see the current step of your payout on your selling dashboard and your
transactions page. **A payout marked as pending, due or held is not money in
your account.** It only becomes paid when an administrator records the
transfer with its bank reference, and your dashboard will then say *Paid out*.

If your payout is held, the reason is recorded against it. Ask us what you need
to do about it.
